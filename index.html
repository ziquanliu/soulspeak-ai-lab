Research questions

When does AI take a shortcut?

We trace when a model settles on an answer and test whether its written reasoning reflects how it got there.

When should we trust its confidence?

We study whether a model’s confidence matches its chance of being right, even after someone challenges its answer.

How can people steer a model?

We study how to change what a model generates while preserving the behaviours people already rely on.
